FROSTREAVER TUI TARGET
======================

This mockup reflects the composite 3-screen model:
- Overview: command center with a collapsible character section and adaptive side rail
- Tactical: map + spawn list + named/nav rail on one screen
- Debug: raw inspection surface for target and memory details

Behavior:
- The side rail resizes to the amount of information shown instead of staying on fixed percentages.
- The selected character summary lives on the Overview screen; it is no longer a dedicated character tab.
- `g` toggles the Groups section on and off.
- `v` toggles the Filters section on and off.
- `z` collapses or expands the currently focused sidebar section.
- `Tab` cycles the focus between Fleet, Character, Groups, Filters, Combat, and Session.
