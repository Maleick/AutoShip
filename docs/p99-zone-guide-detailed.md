# P99 Wiki Zone Guide -- Detailed Reference for Frostreaver TLP

## Source: Project 1999 Per-Level Hunting Guide + Treasure Hunting Guide

Data extracted from https://wiki.project1999.com/Per-Level_Hunting_Guide and https://wiki.project1999.com/Treasure_Hunting_Guide on 2026-03-26.

This document provides P99-sourced zone data organized for a 36-box (6 groups of 6) setup on the Frostreaver TLP server (Classic+Kunark+Velious start, free trade, randomized loot, encounter locking, no truebox). Focus is on GROUP-viable zones that can support MULTIPLE groups simultaneously.

### How to Read This Guide

- **ZEM** = Zone Experience Modifier. 100% is baseline. Higher = faster XP per kill.
- **Era**: Cla = Classic, Kun = Kunark, Vel = Velious
- **Multi-Group Capacity** is estimated based on zone size, spawn density, and number of distinct camp areas.
- **Loot Quality** reflects both vendor trash value and notable rare drops.

## LEVEL 1-10: Starter Phase

### Top Picks for Multi-Group (prioritized)

#### 1. Crushbone (ZEM 213%, Classic) -- BEST IN SLOT

- **Level Range:** Group 7-15, Solo 7-10
- **Area:** Entry area, slave area, trainer hill
- **Mobs:** Orcs
- **Multi-Group Capacity:** 2 groups (entry + trainer hill/throne area)
- **XP Quality:** FAST (213% ZEM is extremely high for this level range)
- **Loot:** Crushbone Belts (quest turn-in for XP), DVinn drops at higher levels
- **Notes:** Save belts for Crushbone Belts Quest. THE best XP/hour for this bracket due to massive ZEM bonus.

#### 2. Befallen (ZEM 213%, Classic) -- EXCELLENT

- **Level Range:** Group 6-15, Solo 6-15
- **Area:** Entrance (6-11), past first locked door (8-15), deeper floors (15-20)
- **Mobs:** Undead assorted
- **Multi-Group Capacity:** 2 groups (entrance + deeper areas)
- **XP Quality:** FAST (213% ZEM, matching Crushbone)
- **Loot:** Moderate vendor trash
- **Notes:** Need Splintered Wooden Key for deeper floors. Named mob Gynok Moltor patrols.

#### 3. Kurn's Tower (ZEM 200%, Kunark) -- EXCELLENT

- **Level Range:** Group 9-24, Solo 9-24
- **Area:** Full tower, multiple floors
- **Mobs:** Skeletons (non-social), assorted undead
- **Multi-Group Capacity:** 3 groups (lower floors / mid floors / upper + basement)
- **XP Quality:** FAST (200% ZEM, covers a HUGE level range 9-24)
- **Loot:** Iksar Berserker Club (rare, valuable), bone chips
- **Notes:** Skeletons are NOT social -- makes pulling easy. One of the best zones in the game for this range. Basement has An undead jester for IBC farming.

#### 4. The Warrens (ZEM 200%, Warrens era) -- EXCELLENT IF AVAILABLE

- **Level Range:** Group 4-10, Solo 4-10
- **Area:** Paineel zoneline
- **Mobs:** Kobolds
- **Multi-Group Capacity:** 1-2 groups
- **XP Quality:** FAST (200% ZEM)
- **Notes:** Check if available on Frostreaver TLP.

#### 5. Blackburrow (ZEM 133%, Classic) -- GOOD

- **Level Range:** Group 5-12, Solo 5-12
- **Area:** Top level (5-8), past first door (8-12), deep area around Lord Elgnub (17-22)
- **Mobs:** Gnolls
- **Multi-Group Capacity:** 2 groups (top + deeper)
- **XP Quality:** MEDIUM-FAST (133% ZEM)
- **Loot:** Gnoll Fangs for Gnoll Bounty or Moonstones quests
- **Notes:** Trains are COMMON. Multiple levels of depth = multiple camps.

#### 6. Field of Bone (ZEM 100%, Kunark) -- GOOD

- **Level Range:** 1-10
- **Area:** Near Cabilis, spiderling cave (4-6)
- **Mobs:** Assorted critters, spiderlings
- **Multi-Group Capacity:** 3+ groups (huge outdoor zone)
- **XP Quality:** MEDIUM (100% ZEM but enormous spawn volume)
- **Loot:** Spiderling silk (sell to players for tailoring)
- **Notes:** Massive zone handles many groups easily. No bottleneck on spawns.

### Multi-Group Strategy (1-10)

Split 6 groups across: Crushbone (2 groups), Kurn's Tower (2 groups), Field of Bone (2 groups). All three handle multiple groups and have excellent ZEM or spawn density. Rotate to Befallen if Crushbone is somehow full.

## LEVEL 10-20: Dungeon Transition

### Top Picks for Multi-Group (prioritized)

#### 1. Estate of Unrest (ZEM 173%, Classic) -- BEST IN SLOT

- **Level Range:** Group 10-28, Solo varies
- **Area:** Entrance/yard trash (10-15), Fireplace (21-28), Basement (24-34)
- **Mobs:** Assorted undead and living
- **Multi-Group Capacity:** 3 groups (yard + fireplace + basement at higher levels)
- **XP Quality:** FAST (173% ZEM, massive level range)
- **Loot:** Various named drops
- **Notes:** THE classic dungeon for this range. Multiple distinct camp areas.

#### 2. Kurn's Tower (ZEM 200%, Kunark) -- continued from above

- **Level Range:** 9-24 (still going strong)
- **Multi-Group Capacity:** 3 groups across floors
- **XP Quality:** FAST
- **Notes:** Best value proposition in the game. 200% ZEM from 9 to 24.

#### 3. Upper Guk (ZEM 166%, Classic) -- VERY GOOD

- **Level Range:** Group 14-20+, Solo varies
- **Area:** Near zone-in (16-20), Squire Camp (20), swampwater crocodiles (10-15)
- **Mobs:** Frogloks, crocodiles
- **Multi-Group Capacity:** 2 groups (upper areas + deeper pulls)
- **XP Quality:** FAST (166% ZEM)
- **Loot:** Runed Bone Fork (rare from Gaz Squire), spell components
- **Notes:** Transitions into Lower Guk at 25+.

#### 4. Highpass Hold (ZEM 107%, Classic) -- DECENT

- **Level Range:** Group 15-20
- **Mobs:** Orcs and Gnolls
- **Multi-Group Capacity:** 1-2 groups
- **XP Quality:** MEDIUM

#### 5. Permafrost (ZEM 120%, Classic) -- DECENT

- **Level Range:** Group 15-20 (near zone-in, ice goblins)
- **Multi-Group Capacity:** 1-2 groups at entry
- **XP Quality:** MEDIUM-FAST
- **Notes:** Mobs in pairs -- need CC or zone-line splitting. Good for monks.

## LEVEL 20-35: The Long Grind

### Top Picks for Multi-Group (prioritized)

#### 1. Lower Guk (ZEM 107%, Classic) -- MASSIVE CAPACITY

- **Level Range:** Group 35-50+ (dead/live sides)
- **Area:** Zoneline to bedroom (35-36), Ritualist/Zols/Dars (40-45), Dead side (45-50), Live side Noble Room (45-50)
- **Mobs:** Frogloks
- **Multi-Group Capacity:** 4+ groups (numerous distinct camps across live/dead sides)
- **XP Quality:** MEDIUM (107% ZEM but incredible mob density)
- **Loot:** Flowing Black Silk Sash (FBSS), White Gold Necklace, fine steel weapons
- **Notes:** On randomized loot server, every named kill is a lottery ticket. Massive dungeon with many camp spots.

#### 2. Cazic Thule (ZEM 113-150%\*, Classic) -- VERY GOOD

- **Level Range:** Group 27-35
- **Area:** Alligator Pit (27-33), Maze (27-33), near Temple (27-33), Alligator Alley (29-34)
- **Mobs:** Alligators, assorted
- **Multi-Group Capacity:** 3 groups (pit + maze + temple area)
- **XP Quality:** FAST (ZEM was boosted significantly)
- **Loot:** Lizard Blood Potion (rare from Cazic Cenobite), various
- **Notes:** P99 wiki notes ZEM "definitely got a boost" -- XP is very good here. Beware see-invis mobs.

#### 3. Crystal Caverns (ZEM 113%, Velious) -- VERY GOOD

- **Level Range:** Group 31-45
- **Area:** Top-down Ry'Gorr orcs (31-39), Geonid tunnel (36-45)
- **Mobs:** Ry'Gorr orcs, Geonids
- **Multi-Group Capacity:** 2-3 groups (orcs + geonids + deeper areas)
- **XP Quality:** FAST (113% dungeon ZEM)
- **Loot:** Velium ore (tradeskill), Geonids drop chipped gems (25-50pp each)
- **Notes:** Dwarf city in zone for banking/vendoring (sneak or faction). NEW with Velious.

#### 4. Tower of Frozen Shadow (ZEM 113%, Velious) -- VERY GOOD

- **Level Range:** Group 27-39
- **Area:** First level right hallways (27-31), entry room and beyond (28-33), second level exit hallway (35-39)
- **Mobs:** Shadow beasts, icy shades, shadowbones, ghostly scholars
- **Multi-Group Capacity:** 2-3 groups (spread across floors)
- **XP Quality:** FAST (113% ZEM, undead = cleric-friendly)
- **Loot:** Floor-specific named drops
- **Notes:** Multi-floor dungeon. Undead mobs good for CLR/NEC. Mobs can spawn "a large undead gnoll" on death.

#### 5. Estate of Unrest (ZEM 173%, Classic) -- still going

- **Level Range:** Basement (24-34), Festering Hag tower (34-40)
- **Multi-Group Capacity:** 2 groups in this range
- **XP Quality:** FAST
- **Loot:** Bloodstained Tunic, Bloodstained Mantle (rare from undead knight)

#### 6. Najena (ZEM 159%, Classic) -- GOOD

- **Level Range:** Group 20-28
- **Area:** Crystal Room (20-26), Pond near Najena's room (22-28), Spider caves (23-25)
- **Mobs:** Magicians, skeletons, spiders, goblins
- **Multi-Group Capacity:** 2 groups (crystal room + pond/spider area)
- **XP Quality:** FAST (159% ZEM)
- **Loot:** Mage focus item components (Broom, Shovel, Stein, Torch)

#### 7. Mistmoore (ZEM 120%, Classic) -- GOOD

- **Level Range:** Group 21-38
- **Area:** Entrance (21), Graveyard (29-32), Castle Entrance (33-38)
- **Multi-Group Capacity:** 2-3 groups
- **XP Quality:** MEDIUM-FAST
- **Loot:** Hooded Black Cloak (rare, valuable)

#### 8. Solusek's Eye / Sol A (ZEM 173%, Classic) -- GOOD

- **Level Range:** Group 32-42
- **Area:** First goblin room (32-35), path to keep (40-43)
- **Mobs:** Goblins, clockworks, gnomes
- **Multi-Group Capacity:** 2 groups
- **XP Quality:** FAST (173% ZEM)
- **Loot:** FS Weapons, HQ Ore

#### 9. Great Divide (ZEM 100-110%, Velious) -- OUTDOOR OPTION

- **Level Range:** 25-43
- **Area:** Tizmak cave (33-37), bear caves (39-43), frost giant area
- **Mobs:** Tizmaks, kodiaks, frost giants
- **Multi-Group Capacity:** 3+ groups (huge outdoor zone)
- **XP Quality:** MEDIUM
- **Loot:** Velium weapons/leather armor (vendor well), Coldain quest items
- **Notes:** Can bind/sell at nearby Thurgadin.

#### 10. Warsliks Woods (ZEM 100%, Kunark)

- **Level Range:** Group 21-34
- **Area:** Forest giant fort, Forest Giant Hut (#8), caves leading to Dalnir
- **Mobs:** Forest giants, skulking brutes
- **Multi-Group Capacity:** 2 groups (giant fort + Dalnir caves)
- **XP Quality:** MEDIUM
- **Loot:** Coins and gems from skulking brutes

## LEVEL 35-50: Mid-Game Dungeons

### Top Picks for Multi-Group (prioritized)

#### 1. City of Mist (ZEM 113-125%, Kunark) -- BEST IN SLOT

- **Level Range:** Group 41-51
- **Area:** Entrance (41-43), Stables (42-46), Temple (43-49), Arena (44-50), Moat (45-51), 2nd Floor (49-54)
- **Mobs:** Golems (Fog > Mist > Vapor > Haze), ghosts
- **Multi-Group Capacity:** 4+ groups (entrance, stables, temple, arena, moat are all distinct camps)
- **XP Quality:** FAST (113-125% ZEM depending on area)
- **Loot:** Wraith Bone Hammer, Jade Reaver, quest items
- **Notes:** Ghost mobs proc lifetaps. Massive dungeon with many floors/areas = perfect for multi-group.

#### 2. The Hole (ZEM 171-200%, Classic) -- EXCELLENT

- **Level Range:** Group 41-59
- **Area:** Entrance elementals (41-50), Drop Down/Castle (50-54), Docks/Town (55-59), Undead Crypt (55-60), Yael Pit (55-59)
- **Mobs:** Elementals (summoned), undead, rock golems
- **Multi-Group Capacity:** 3-4 groups (entrance, castle, town, undead tower)
- **XP Quality:** VERY FAST (171-200% ZEM -- one of the highest in the game)
- **Loot:** Loam Encrusted gear, Idol of Underking
- **Notes:** Enormous level range (41-60). Elementals at entrance are 39-40 and come in multiples. Castle requires gate/OT hammer for escape. Undead crypt is excellent for CLR at 55-60. 200% ZEM in deeper areas.

#### 3. Solusek's Eye / Sol A (ZEM 173%, Classic) -- continued

- **Level Range:** Group 38-43
- **Area:** Path to keep (clockworks and gnomes)
- **Multi-Group Capacity:** 1-2 groups
- **XP Quality:** FAST

#### 4. Lower Guk (ZEM 107%, Classic) -- continued

- **Level Range:** Group 40-50
- **Area:** Ritualist (40-45), Dead Side (45-50), Noble Room (45-50)
- **Multi-Group Capacity:** 3+ groups
- **Loot:** FBSS, White Gold Necklace

#### 5. Kedge Keep (ZEM 217%, Classic) -- HIGHEST ZEM IN GAME

- **Level Range:** Group 40-59
- **Mobs:** Fish, sharks, seahorses, mermaids, piranha
- **Multi-Group Capacity:** 2 groups
- **XP Quality:** VERY FAST (217% ZEM!)
- **Loot:** Various underwater drops
- **Notes:** Enduring Breath REQUIRED. CC recommended for breaking schools of fish. Highest ZEM in the game but underwater combat is painful. Animal charming works on fish/sharks/seahorses.

#### 6. Nagafen's Lair / SolB (ZEM ~100-115%, Classic)

- **Level Range:** Group 45-56
- **Area:** BNB (Bats and Bugs), Efreeti Room, Lava Duct Crawlers (51-59)
- **Mobs:** Bats, bugs, fire giants, efreeti, lava duct crawlers
- **Multi-Group Capacity:** 2-3 groups
- **XP Quality:** MEDIUM
- **Loot:** Golden Efreeti Boots, Cloak of Shadows, Mithril armor
- **Notes:** Lava duct crawlers proc Boiling Blood (blocks regen spells).

#### 7. Eastern Wastes (ZEM 100%, Velious) -- OUTDOOR

- **Level Range:** Group 39-49
- **Area:** Orc fort (40-45), Frost Giant Fort (40-45), Mercenary Ruins (44-49), animal fields (39-44)
- **Mobs:** Ry'Gorr Orcs, Frost Giants, animals, Ulthorks
- **Multi-Group Capacity:** 3+ groups (huge outdoor zone with distinct areas)
- **XP Quality:** MEDIUM
- **Loot:** Frost giant faction drops, animal charming
- **Notes:** Coldain will assist on giant fights. Mammoth/rhino/giant progression.

#### 8. Frontier Mountains (ZEM 100%, Kunark)

- **Level Range:** Group 31-40
- **Area:** Giant Fort
- **Mobs:** Mountain Giant Tumps and Hillocks
- **Multi-Group Capacity:** 2 groups
- **XP Quality:** MEDIUM

#### 9. Temple of Droga (ZEM 100%, Kunark)

- **Level Range:** Group 39-45
- **Area:** Hallway between areas 2 and 3, Chief room
- **Mobs:** Goblins (shamans + harm touching SK mobs)
- **Multi-Group Capacity:** 2 groups
- **Notes:** One-way drop after zone-in -- melees need to fight or sneak out.

#### 10. Permafrost (ZEM 120-225%, Classic)

- **Level Range:** 40-56
- **Area:** Ice Giants (40-48), Spiders (49-56), Polar Bear Pits (56-59)
- **Multi-Group Capacity:** 2 groups
- **XP Quality:** MEDIUM to VERY FAST (225% ZEM for spiders/bear pits!)
- **Loot:** Ice Giant Toes (20-30pp to players), coin
- **Notes:** Bear pits at 225% ZEM are incredible for 56-59.

## LEVEL 50-60: Endgame Push

### Top Picks for Multi-Group (prioritized)

#### 1. Old Sebilis (ZEM 113%, Kunark) -- THE endgame dungeon

- **Level Range:** Group 53-60
- **Mobs:** Frogloks, fungus men, juggs
- **Multi-Group Capacity:** 5-6 groups (disco 1, disco 2, left wing, crypt, juggs, myconids)
- **XP Quality:** FAST
- **Loot:** Box of Nil Space, Cone of Mystics, Bonecaster's Robe, Hierophant's Cloak, Runebranded Girdle, gems (~400pp/hr from gem camp)
- **Notes:** Requires key. Can absorb entire 36-box operation across its many wings.

#### 2. Kael Drakkel (ZEM 113%, Velious) -- ARMOR + XP

- **Level Range:** Group 50-60
- **Area:** Arena, armor drop areas, dire wolves (55-59)
- **Mobs:** Frost Giants, dire wolves
- **Multi-Group Capacity:** 3-6 groups
- **XP Quality:** FAST
- **Loot:** Thurgadin armor templates, quest gems, named drops
- **Notes:** Arena is 56-59. Dire wolves are animal-charmable but extremely dangerous.

#### 3. The Hole (ZEM 200%, Classic) -- continued

- **Level Range:** Group 50-59
- **Area:** Castle, Docks/Town, Undead Tower, Undead Crypt, Yael Pit
- **Multi-Group Capacity:** 3-4 groups
- **XP Quality:** VERY FAST (200% ZEM)
- **Loot:** Loam Encrusted gear, elementals
- **Notes:** Castle starts greening at upper 50s but still good XP. Undead crypt (55-60) is excellent for CLR.

#### 4. Howling Stones / Charasis (ZEM 113%, Kunark)

- **Level Range:** Group 51-59
- **Area:** Entrance (51-56), Basement (53-59), North (56-59), West (57-59), South (58-59)
- **Mobs:** Undead, assorted
- **Multi-Group Capacity:** 3 groups (entrance, basement, north/west/south wings)
- **XP Quality:** FAST
- **Loot:** Hand of the Reaper, Fingerbone Hoop, Enshrouded Veil, Golden Bracer
- **Notes:** Requires Key to Charasis. South wing is most profitable. Lowers Venril Sathir faction.

#### 5. Chardok (ZEM 113%, Kunark)

- **Level Range:** Group 52-59
- **Area:** Entrance/Betrayer/Waterfall, Exit, Betrayer Camp
- **Mobs:** Sarnaks, sarnak dogs
- **Multi-Group Capacity:** 3 groups
- **XP Quality:** FAST
- **Loot:** Incarnadine BP/Greaves, Vibrating Gauntlets of Infuse, Fancy Velvet Mantle, pipe for Monk epic
- **Notes:** Entrance is not exit -- watch for trains.

#### 6. Siren's Grotto (ZEM 113%, Velious)

- **Level Range:** Group 57-59
- **Area:** Arena (#3) - Manatee/Walrus servants
- **Mobs:** Enthralled creatures, swordfish, coldspine seahorses
- **Multi-Group Capacity:** 2 groups
- **XP Quality:** FAST
- **Loot:** Good chance of drops from arena
- **Notes:** Requires CLR or Torpor SHM for heals, ENC/BRD for CC, MNK for pulls.

#### 7. Velketor's Labyrinth (ZEM 113%, Velious)

- **Level Range:** Group 57-59
- **Area:** Entrance area
- **Mobs:** Kobolds, spiders
- **Multi-Group Capacity:** 2-3 groups
- **XP Quality:** FAST
- **Loot:** Spider Fur Belt/Collar/Cloak, Lute of the Howler

#### 8. Burning Woods (ZEM 100%, Kunark)

- **Level Range:** Solo 50-56
- **Area:** Haunted Ruins (50-52), Giant Fort (51-56), near Skyfire zoneline (51-56)
- **Mobs:** Skeletons, gorillas, Forest Giant Verdant/Ancient, Sarnak Zealots
- **Multi-Group Capacity:** 2 groups (ruins + giant fort)
- **XP Quality:** MEDIUM
- **Loot:** Giant Foreman's Tunic, Giant Lord's Tunic, Forest Loop, weapons vendor 10-45pp

#### 9. Karnor's Castle (ZEM 113%, Kunark)

- **Level Range:** Group 52-59
- **Area:** Entrance/Moat (52-59), Left Courtyard (54-58)
- **Mobs:** Skeletal guardians, various
- **Multi-Group Capacity:** 3 groups
- **XP Quality:** FAST
- **Loot:** KC-specific drops

#### 10. Kedge Keep (ZEM 217%, Classic) -- NICHE BUT INCREDIBLE ZEM

- **Level Range:** 52-59
- **Area:** Deep rooms
- **XP Quality:** VERY FAST (217% ZEM)
- **Notes:** Enduring Breath required. Animal charming works. Dispel is common from mobs.

## CRITICAL NOTES FOR 36-BOX OPERATION

### Zones That Can Support 4+ Groups Simultaneously

These are your primary targets -- zones large enough that 18-36 characters won't exhaust spawns:

1. **Old Sebilis** (50-60): 5-6 distinct camp areas. Can absorb all 36 characters.
2. **City of Mist** (41-54): 4-5 distinct areas across multiple floors.
3. **Lower Guk** (35-50): 4+ camps across live/dead sides, tunnels.
4. **Kael Drakkel** (48-60): Massive zone, arena + multiple named areas.
5. **The Hole** (41-60): Entrance, castle, town, undead tower, Yael pit.
6. **Cazic Thule** (27-35): Pit, maze, temple, alligator alley.
7. **Eastern Wastes** (39-49): Orc fort, giant fort, mercenary ruins, animal fields.
8. **Field of Bone** (1-10): Huge outdoor zone.
9. **Great Divide** (25-43): Tizmak caves, bear caves, giant areas.
10. **Karnor's Castle** (40-59): Entrance, moat, courtyards.
