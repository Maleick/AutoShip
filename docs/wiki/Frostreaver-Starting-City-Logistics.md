# Frostreaver Starting City Logistics

Planning reference for `TextQuest#1587`. This page turns the locked city allocations from the Jeff 2026-04-14 session into a concrete launch-day race/class matrix, early leveling route, and merge plan that fits the finalized 36-character roster from [Frostreaver Farming & XP Guide](Frostreaver-Farming-Guide.md).

## Planning Assumptions

- The class totals are fixed by the roster in [Frostreaver Farming & XP Guide](Frostreaver-Farming-Guide.md).
- Frostreaver launches with Standard + Velious unlocked, Beastlords available at launch, Free Trade on, Randomized Loot on, Encounter Locking on, Live-speed XP, and Legacy Characters enabled.
- The GFay cohort stays self-contained through the starter range.
- Neriak is a feeder cohort into the Freeport side, not a permanent standalone six-box.
- Iksar remain the faction-friction cohort in Antonica, so Cabilis handoffs should route through neutral docks and mules instead of the Freeport bank loop.

## Why This Split Works

- `CAB-A` and `CAB-B` are both real day-one leveling boxes: tank / slow / melee or pet / slow / melee. They can stay in Kunark until Kurn's value starts to flatten.
- `GFY-A` is intentionally self-contained and should not be forced to boat early. It already has heals, CC, pull control, and enough utility to own Crushbone.
- `NER-A` is intentionally temporary. Dark Elf travel into East Commonlands is short, so this cohort should collapse into the Freeport side as soon as spellbooks and starter weapons are done.
- `FP-A` is the stable Human core from minute one. `FP-B` becomes clean once the Erudite paladin / cleric pair finishes the Qeynos-side transfer.
- The final roster anchors stay compatible with the six-group end-state from `#1521`, so no city choice creates a dead-end class mix later.

## Leveling and Convergence Plan

Operationally there are five travel origins because the Freeport cluster includes a two-character Erudin satellite.

| Origin | Levels 1-4 | Levels 4-8 | Levels 8-12 | Travel to Merge |
| ------ | ---------- | ---------- | ----------- | --------------- |
| Cabilis | Field of Bone | Field of Bone / Kurn's entry | Kurn's Tower | Field of Bone -> Lake of Ill Omen -> Frontier Mountains -> Firiona Vie docks -> boat to Butcherblock |
| Freeport Human core | Freeport newbie yards | East Commonlands orcs / dervs | East Commonlands / West Commonlands / Befallen staging | East Freeport dock -> boat to Butcherblock |
| Erudin satellite | Toxxulia Forest / Erudin yard | Boat to Qeynos side, then Qeynos Hills / Blackburrow | Karanas -> East Commonlands catch-up | Once linked with the Freeport core, use the same East Freeport -> Butcherblock boat |
| Neriak feeder | Nektulos Forest | East Commonlands with the Freeport core | East Commonlands / West Commonlands / Befallen staging | Same East Freeport -> Butcherblock boat after the EC merge |
| Greater Faydark | Greater Faydark ruins / orc hill | Crushbone entrance | Crushbone deeper / Greater Faydark cleanup | Walk to Butcherblock when the all-roster move is called |

### Earliest Practical Full-Roster Convergence

**Level target:** 12  
**Staging zone:** Butcherblock Mountains docks  
**First shared XP target:** Estate of Unrest yard / entry

This is the earliest point where the convergence is still efficient instead of merely possible.

- Cabilis loses too much value if it abandons Kurn's before roughly 10-12.
- GFay is already one zone away from the Unrest door.
- Freeport and Neriak can share the East Commonlands / East Freeport ramp-up and arrive together.
- Butcherblock is the only clean dock-side rendezvous that is adjacent to both the GFay cohort and the first shared dungeon target.

### Partial Convergence Points

- **Level 4-6:** Neriak should already be absorbed into the Freeport side in East Commonlands.
- **Level 8-10:** The Human core, Dark Elf feeder, and GFay group can all be on Faydwer if needed, but doing that before the Cabilis group is ready is lower value than letting Cabilis finish Kurn's.
- **Level 12+:** Re-sort from city boxes into final raid groups while moving through Unrest, Upper Guk, and the normal 10-20 split.

## Gear Handoff Logistics

- Use **East Commonlands tunnel** as the Antonica distribution hub for the Freeport Human core, the Dark Elf feeder, and the Erudite satellite once it reaches the east side.
- Use **Butcherblock docks** as the Faydwer receiving hub and the safest pre-Unrest rendezvous for GFay and any boat arrivals.
- Use **Firiona Vie docks** as the Kunark export point. Cabilis should route gear out through Firiona Vie and non-Iksar mules instead of trying to make Freeport the primary bank loop.
- Do not plan on Cabilis banking in Freeport. Treat Iksar travel through Antonica as a straight dock-to-dock transfer unless faction work is already done.
- On a Free Trade server, the first cross-city packages should be low-friction items: banded sets, bronze weapons, 4-8 slot bags, rawhide / patchwork fillers, and complete spell sets.
- Prioritize the first upgrades in this order: monks and plate tanks, then shamans / clerics, then bard utility gear and weight-reduction bags.
- Treat Legacy heirloom as the exception path for odd no-drop cases. Free Trade should handle almost all launch-day handoff needs by itself.

## Legacy XP Strategy

Legacy Characters matter more for account planning than for day-one travel. The recommended policy for these 36 accounts is:

| Phase | Trigger | Account Policy |
| ----- | ------- | -------------- |
| Seed phase | Launch through first cap | Each listed launch character is that account's Legacy seed. Do not detour into alts before the seed hits the current era cap. |
| First bonus | Seed reaches cap | Mark the account as `+10% ready` and keep leveling the main roster until the core six groups are stable. |
| Catch-up phase | Main is capped and frequently idle | Only then create or activate a same-account alt / persona. Prioritize healer, shaman, bard, and tank accounts first because they gain the most from faster catch-up when caps move. |
| Stack discipline | Two or more capped characters on one account | Stop chasing stacks just because they exist. A second capped character for `+20%` is useful; racing toward the full `+100%` cap is not a launch priority for this 36-account plan. |
| Expansion rollover | Level cap increases | Re-cap the seed character first. Legacy stacks disappear until that account has a character at the new era cap again. |

Additional rules worth keeping explicit:

- The Frostreaver vote locked Legacy Characters on.
- Legacy bonuses stack with other XP bonuses, including zone-based bonus systems and potions.
- Legacy is best treated as a **catch-up accelerator** after the launch mains are secure, not as a reason to split attention during the first merge window.

## Operational Notes

- If launch-day home-city selection lets the Erudite paladin / cleric pair start in Freeport, take it and keep the rest of this plan unchanged.
- If not, the Erudite pair is the only intentional long-haul transfer in the matrix. Keep the count small for that reason.
- If the Erudite transfer becomes a real drag in practice, the lowest-risk swap is `Erudite Paladin -> Human Paladin` and `Erudite Cleric -> Human Cleric`. The class totals stay unchanged and every other section of this plan still works.
