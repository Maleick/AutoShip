# Pre-Launch Leveling Guide (1-65)

**Operator-facing consolidated guide for Frostreaver TLP multibox progression.**

This document merges per-era zone research into a single reference. Source
documents: [P99 Zone Guide](P99-Zone-Guide.md),
[Frostreaver Farming Guide](Frostreaver-Farming-Guide.md), and
[Research: Test-Server Leveling Paths and Overlooked Zones](Research-Test-Server-Leveling-Paths.md).

> **Frostreaver TLP note:** Frostreaver launches at Velious with Free Trade,
> Randomized Loot, Encounter Locking, and No Truebox. Classic and Kunark
> content is present from day 1. Level cap at launch is 60 (Velious). The
> 60-65 section covers content unlocked when Luclin/PoP are released.

---

## How to Read This Guide

- **ZEM** = Zone Experience Modifier (100% = baseline outdoor XP). Higher is faster.
- **Pick-eligible** = zone reliably generates `/pickzone` instances when a second group
  enters. Marked with **[PICK]**.
- **Multi-Group Capacity** = how many groups can camp the zone simultaneously without
  spawn contention.
- **Automation Safety** = suitability for unattended TextQuest automation (High / Medium / Low).
- Era tags: **[Classic]** **[Kunark]** **[Velious]** **[Luclin]** **[PoP]**

## Recommended 1-65 Route

This is the primary recommendation. Alternatives and off-meta options are
detailed in per-era sections below.

| Levels | Zone                  | Notes                                              |
| ------ | --------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| 1-10   | Field of Bone         | High ZEM, outdoor, handles 2-3 groups              |
| 10-24  | Kurn's Tower          | ZEM 200%, skeletons non-social, 3 group capacity   |
| 20-35  | Crystal Caverns       | ZEM 113%, stable orc/geonid camps, low traffic     |
| 20-35  | Crypt of Dalnir       | Off-meta backup, high ZEM, antisocial dungeon      |
| 26-39  | The Overthere         | Outdoor fallback, easy pull lines, low competition |
| 35-50  | City of Mist          | ZEM 113-125%, 3-4 group capacity, excellent loot   |
| 44-60  | Chardok               | ZEM 113%, pick-eligible, reliable open camps       |
| 50-60  | Old Sebilis            | ZEM 113%, 4-6 group capacity, best Kunark XP       |
| 60     | Kael Drakkel (cap)    | Armor quest drops + XP buffer at 60                |
| 60-65  | Planes of Power (PoP) | Available after Luclin/PoP unlock                  |

**Off-meta outdoor pivot (automation-first):**
Steamfont/Misty Thicket (1-12) -> Warsliks Woods (20-26) ->
The Overthere (31-39) -> Crystal Caverns (27-40) -> Chardok (44-60).

## Level 1-10: Starter Phase

Frostreaver starts at Velious, but Classic and Kunark newbie zones are open.
Split 6 groups across 2-3 starter zones to avoid spawn contention.

### Primary Picks

| Zone                 | ZEM  | Era       | Level Range | Multi-Group | Automation Safety | Pick-eligible | Notes                                        |
| -------------------- | ---- | --------- | ----------- | ----------- | ----------------- | ------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Field of Bone        | 100% | [Kunark]  | 1-15        | 2-3 groups  | High              | No            | Pit area 5-10, skeletons/wolves 1-5          |
| Crushbone            | 213% | [Classic] | 7-15        | 1-2 groups  | Medium            | No            | THE starter dungeon; throne room 10-15       |
| Befallen             | 213% | [Classic] | 6-20        | 1-2 groups  | Medium            | No            | Incredible ZEM but narrow layout             |
| Kurn's Tower (lower) | 200% | [Kunark]  | 9-24        | 3 groups    | High              | No            | Skeletons are non-social; pulls are clean    |
| Blackburrow          | 133% | [Classic] | 5-22        | 1-2 groups  | Medium            | No            | Multi-level; slower pull management          |

### Off-Meta Picks

| Zone                    | ZEM  | Era       | Level Range | Multi-Group | Automation Safety | Pick-eligible |
| ----------------------- | ---- | --------- | ----------- | ----------- | ----------------- | ------------- |
| Steamfont Mountains     | 100% | [Classic] | 1-12        | Outdoor     | High              | No            |
| Misty Thicket           | 100% | [Classic] | 1-12        | Outdoor     | High              | No            |
| Racial newbie zones     | 100% | various   | 1-10        | Split       | High              | No            |

**Strategy (1-10):** Kurn's Tower is the standout -- covers this range AND the
next. Crushbone/Befallen are fastest for 1-3 groups but quickly fill. For
automation safety with 6 groups, split Field of Bone (2-3 groups) + Kurn's
Tower (1 group starting early) + one racial newbie zone (remainder).

The longest stretch. Primary path: Crystal Caverns -> City of Mist -> Chardok/Sebilis.

| Crystal Caverns       | 113%     | [Velious] | 27-40       | 2-3 groups  | Medium-High       | Low-Medium    | Continued from earlier bracket                               |
| Nagafen's Lair (SolB) | 100-115% | [Classic] | 35-50       | 1-2 groups  | Medium            | No            | Fire giants and bats; Efreeti Boots camp                     |

**Strategy (30-50):** City of Mist is the centerpiece -- spread 3-4 groups
across courtyard and inner city. Karnor's Castle handles overflow. SolB hosts
1-2 plat-rotation groups at the Efreeti camp. The Hole is an option only with
attended supervision due to vertical geometry.

## Level 60-65: Post-Velious Expansion

Available after Luclin and/or Planes of Power unlock on Frostreaver. Luclin
raises the cap to 65.

| Zone                        | Era      | Level Range | Multi-Group | Notes                                              |
| --------------------------- | -------- | ----------- | ----------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Planes of Power (PoP)       | [PoP]    | 60-65       | Varies      | Tier 1-2 planes via flagging; primary 60-65 path   |
| Sanctus Seru                | [Luclin] | 55-65       | 2-3 groups  | Good Luclin XP, non-faction content available      |
| Akheva Ruins                | [Luclin] | 58-65       | 2-3 groups  | Dense spawns, high XP; camp management required    |
| Vex Thal                    | [Luclin] | 60-65       | Raid-only   | Raid zone; not a leveling path                     |
| Echo Caverns                | [Luclin] | 58-65       | 2 groups    | Shissar; quieter option when Akheva is camped      |

**Strategy (60-65):** This era is not yet validated for Frostreaver. Flag into
PoP tier 1 planes (Tactics, Innovation, Storms, Valor) as quickly as possible
for best XP density. Luclin outdoor and dungeon XP is competitive but PoP
content is the standard path for this bracket on Live TLP servers.

## Pick-Eligible Zone Summary

Zones with reliable `/pickzone` availability. Use picks to isolate groups from
competition. Note: empty zones do NOT generate picks -- a zone needs traffic to
spawn a new instance.

| Zone       | Era       | Level Range | Pick Reliability | Notes                                         |
| ---------- | --------- | ----------- | ---------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Chardok    | [Kunark]  | 44-60       | Medium-High      | Best overall pick candidate; open camps noted |
| Crystal Caverns | [Velious] | 27-40  | Low-Medium       | Occasional picks when zone has traffic        |
| Velketor's Labyrinth | [Velious] | 40-60 | Low         | Inner sections; unpredictable                 |

All other zones in this guide are **not pick-eligible** or generate picks too
rarely to rely on. See [Research: Test-Server Leveling Paths](Research-Test-Server-Leveling-Paths.md)
for the negative-context analysis on `/pickzone` traps.

## Zones to Avoid for Unattended Automation

| Zone             | Issue                                                                   |
| ---------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The Hole         | Vertical geometry + expensive recovery; poor unattended default         |
| Temple of Droga  | Runners and healers through walls; bad split cascades                   |
| Kaesora          | Multi-level undead layout; less forgiving than Dalnir or Crystal Caverns|
| Deep Mistmoore   | Difficulty spikes unpredictably in deeper sections                      |
| Western Wastes   | Named pop risk; not best XP; supervision required                       |
