# Research: Sleeper's Tomb Keying Opportunity Window

*Research date: 2026-04-15*

Sleeper's Tomb keying looks like a real but short-lived Frostreaver income stream. The quest itself is simple. The bottleneck is raid kill volume and how quickly other guilds can turn Velious raid kills into keyed rosters. On a free-trade server, the best monetization lane is likely tradable talismans or keyed completion services during the first few lockouts after the server's top raid guilds stabilize Velious dragons.

## What Matters on Frostreaver

The official Frostreaver poll outcome locks in the rules that shape this market:

- **Standard + Scars of Velious launch**
- **Beastlords at launch**
- **No Truebox**
- **Encounter Locking**
- **Live/Fastest XP**
- **Free Trade**
- **Randomized Loot**

Those rules matter because they compress the time between launch and raid readiness:

- Fast XP and no truebox accelerate leveling and gearing.
- Encounter locking reduces open-world race grief and makes organized dragon farming cleaner.
- Free trade creates buyers for anything that can be sold directly.
- Randomized loot floods the broader raid-item market, which pushes value toward gated access, services, and time-saving convenience instead of ordinary drops.

## Sleeper's Tomb Key Quest Chain

This is a one-item access quest, not a long multi-stage key line.

### Turn-in flow

1. Acquire **any one** valid talisman.
2. Reach **Jaled Dar's Shade** in Dragon Necropolis.
3. Hand in a single qualifying talisman.
4. Receive **Sleeper's Key**.

### Getting to Jaled Dar's Shade

Public guides list three ways into the room:

- **Pick lock** the door with roughly **210+ skill**
- Complete the separate **Jaled Dar's Tomb Key** quest
- Use **levitate + roof drop-in** access

### Valid key items

| Item | Source | Standard-server flag | Drop note | Practical lane |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Klandicar's Talisman** | Klandicar, Western Wastes | No Trade | Common | Early dragon farm |
| **Sontalak's Talisman** | Sontalak, Western Wastes | No Trade | Common | Early dragon farm |
| **Lendiniara's Talisman** | Lendiniara the Keeper, Temple of Veeshan | No Trade | Common | ToV lane |
| **Yelinak's Talisman** | Lord Yelinak, Skyshrine | No Trade | Uncommon | Higher-difficulty raid lane |
| **Zlandicar's Talisman** | Zlandicar, Dragon Necropolis | Tradable | Uncommon | Best direct-sale lane |
| **Shard of Hsagra's Talisman** | Derakor, King Tormax, Statue of Rallos Zek, Velketor | No Trade | Common/Uncommon; rare from Velketor | Giant/Velks overflow lane |

### Operational takeaway

The quest complexity is low. The real gating variables are:

- how many key-eligible bosses the roster can kill every reset
- whether Frostreaver allows the no-trade talismans to circulate under Free Trade
- how quickly the server's raid scene saturates access

## Free-Trade Tradeability Matrix

The safest planning assumption is conservative:

- **Zlandicar's Talisman** is definitely tradable because it is tradable even on standard rulesets.
- **Sleeper's Key** itself should be treated as **non-tradable** on Frostreaver until live testing proves otherwise.
- The other talismans and the Hsagra shard are the uncertain middle: they are no-trade on standard servers, and Frostreaver's Free Trade may free them up, but keyed-content exceptions have existed on earlier free-trade TLPs.

| Item class | Standard flag | Frostreaver expectation | Confidence | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Zlandicar's Talisman** | Tradable | **Tradable** | High | Cleanest direct component flip |
| **Klandicar / Sontalak / Lendiniara / Yelinak talismans** | No Trade | **Probably tradable, but not confirmed** | Medium-Low | If tradable, these become the real money lane; if not, monetize via loot rights or in-house allocation |
| **Shard of Hsagra's Talisman** | No Trade | **Probably tradable, but not confirmed** | Medium-Low | Backup supply lane from Kael/Velks kills |
| **Sleeper's Key** | Key item / historically treated as a restricted access object | **Assume non-tradable** | Medium | Market likely forms around components or completion services, not finished keys |

### Why the key is treated as non-tradable

Public Daybreak forum discussion during Teek's free-trade era explicitly called out that **"Plane of Time, Vex Thall and Sleeper's loot is tradable but the keys are not,"** with follow-up posts describing key items that **poof** and convert to achievement-style access rather than remaining tradeable objects. That is enough to reject any plan that depends on selling finished Sleeper's Keys directly.

### Best practical interpretation

- **Direct item resale**: highest confidence on **Zlandicar's Talisman**
- **Possible direct resale after launch verification**: the other talismans and Hsagra shard
- **Service sale instead of item sale**: if no-trade restrictions stay in place, monetize by selling loot rights, boss slots, or completed turn-in assistance instead of the finished key

## Timeline Estimates

These estimates assume:

- raid-style lockouts are effectively a **6.5-day cadence**
- each successful key-target kill produces roughly **one usable key item**
- the roster stabilizes easier key lanes before Yelinak and deep ToV lanes

### Per-character time-to-key

| Path | Time once supply exists | Real bottleneck |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Buy a tradable talisman** | ~15-30 minutes for travel and turn-in | Market supply |
| **Receive an in-house talisman** | Same day as award | Loot priority queue |
| **Loot-rights / service completion** | Same raid night if the seller controls the kill and turn-in flow | Seller availability |

### Full-roster estimate

| Model | First keyed core | 36 keyed chars | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Market-heavy hybrid** | Week 4-5 from launch | Week 6-8 | Requires early tradable talisman supply |
| **Balanced self-farm + market buy** | Week 5-6 | Week 7-10 | Most realistic Frostreaver plan |
| **Self-farm only** | Week 5-6 | Week 8-12 | Depends on reliable weekly dragon/giant cadence |

### Why the window is not instant on Frostreaver

Teek showed that Velious guilds can clear Sleeper's Tomb almost immediately **once Velious is already unlocked and raid-geared guilds are ready**. Frostreaver is different because Velious is live from day one, so the server still has to:

- level to 60
- assemble tank/heal depth
- farm enough Kael/Skyshrine/Western Wastes access targets to make keying routine

That likely creates a few weeks of setup time before ST keying becomes a repeatable business.

## 36-Account Keying Rotation

The goal is not "key all 36 at random." The goal is to get the minimum safe ST-capable spine keyed first, then scale to a full raid.

### Wave 1: Control and survival core (12)

- 3 Warriors
- 1 Shadowknight
- 4 Clerics
- 2 Bards
- 1 Shaman
- 1 Enchanter

This wave gives tank anchors, the first real CH backbone, resist/song coverage, slows, and hard crowd control. It is the minimum intelligent first wave if the plan is to learn ST safely and not just own keys on paper.

### Wave 2: Raid backbone expansion (12)

- 2 Paladins
- 2 Clerics
- 2 Bards
- 2 Shamans
- 4 Monks

This wave adds more tank redundancy, finishes the six-cleric healing shape, and adds the first dense melee DPS block that makes warder-style kill speed realistic.

### Wave 3: Utility and remaining DPS (12)

- 2 Bards
- 1 Shaman
- 4 Monks
- 1 Druid
- 1 Beastlord
- 1 Ranger
- 1 Wizard
- 1 Magician

This completes the full 36-character stack. At this point ST keying is no longer a gating problem for the raid and becomes purely a throughput question.

### Rotation logic by target

1. **Always buy or assign Zlandicar's Talisman first** if it is available at a rational price.
2. **Farm Klandicar and Sontalak every reset** because they are earlier, simpler supply lanes.
3. **Use Derakor / Velketor / Tormax shard paths** as filler supply instead of waiting only on dragon RNG.
4. **Add Yelinak and Lendiniara later** once Skyshrine and ToV lanes are operationally stable.
5. **Sell surplus early pieces** only until Wave 1 is finished. After that, compare sale value against the value of accelerating Wave 2.

### Practical staffing rule

Do **not** spread the first 10-12 keys across vanity alts. Concentrate them in the raid spine, or the roster ends up with many keyed characters but not enough keyed healing/tank/control depth to exploit the zone.

## Market Assessment

Frostreaver already shows visible **GDKP recruitment** before launch. That matters because it confirms a Krono-denominated raid economy will exist from the start, not months later.

At the same time, Teek's free-trade economy shows how fast ordinary raid loot compresses:

- early highly contested items can sell for **multiple Krono**
- once the free-trade/random-loot market matures, most items flatten toward **100-500 plat or 1-2 Krono**

That pushes Sleeper's Tomb keying into a very specific role: it is not a forever-camp. It is a **timing trade**.

### Best monetization lanes

| Lane | Best case | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Tradable talisman flips** | Best margin per raid-hour if Frostreaver frees up the no-trade talismans | Supply spikes and price collapses once several guilds farm them |
| **Zlandicar talisman flips** | Highest-confidence direct sale because the item is already tradable on standard rulesets | Competes with every other DN dragon farm |
| **Key completion service** | Strong fallback if the key stays non-tradable but components or boss slots can still be sold | More labor per sale; needs reputation and scheduling |
| **Keep for internal acceleration** | Strongest long-term roster value if prices fall too fast | Opportunity cost if the market stays hot longer than expected |

### Working price bands

These are **inference bands**, not observed sale logs.

| Window | Tradable talisman | Completed key/service slot | Interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Early premium** | 1-3 Krono | 2-4 Krono | Worth diverting some raid time into sales |
| **Mid window** | 0.5-1.5 Krono | 1-2 Krono | Sell only true surplus |
| **Late convenience** | <=1 Krono | <=1 Krono | Stop treating ST keying as a primary income stream |

### Kill-or-keep rule

If the market opens below these bands, stop selling early and use every piece to accelerate the 36-box raid core instead. The opportunity only exists if key access stays materially more expensive than generic Velious loot.

## Competition Window

### Analog 1: Teek Velious unlock

Teek's public progression page shows:

- **Faceless Fury** cleared Sleeper's Tomb on **2024-08-15**, the same day Velious unlocked.
- **Qeynos Yacht Club** cleared ST by **2024-08-17**.
- By **2024-09-01**, several additional guilds had already cleared it.

That means once a free-trade/random-loot server already has geared raid guilds in position, ST access goes from "elite" to "widely available among the serious raid scene" in **days to a few weeks**, not months.

### Analog 2: Fangbreaker expanded start

Fangbreaker's public progression page is closer to Frostreaver's launch shape:

- server launched **2025-05-28**
- **Unconcerned Citizenz** cleared Sleeper's Tomb on **2025-07-12**
- the same guild finished **Vulak`Aerr** on **2025-07-22**

That puts the first ST clear roughly **45 days after launch** on an expanded-start server. Frostreaver's faster XP and no-truebox rules could compress that even further.

### Frostreaver opportunity window

Best estimate:

- **Launch to week 4**: too early; raid force still stabilizing
- **Weeks 5-8**: premium keying market most likely
- **Weeks 8-10**: competition accelerates hard
- **After week 10**: ST keying likely becomes internal progression or convenience sales, not a standout income stream

If Frostreaver's top guilds outperform the Fangbreaker analog because of fast XP and no truebox, move every one of those windows **1-2 weeks earlier**.

---

## Decision

Treat Sleeper's Tomb keying as a **short, mid-phase monetization spike**, not a whole-era pillar.

Recommended posture:

1. **Pursue it** because the reward-to-complexity ratio is good.
2. **Key Wave 1 immediately** before selling much of anything.
3. **Sell tradable talismans or keyed services during weeks 5-8** if prices open above the working bands.
4. **Pivot to internal acceleration** the moment component prices collapse toward the generic Velious 1-krono floor.

The winning play is not "key everyone first" and not "sell everything." It is **finish the raid spine, sell true surplus while the market is thin, then close the door before competition normalizes access**.
