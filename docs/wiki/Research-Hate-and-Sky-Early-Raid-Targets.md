# Research: Plane of Hate and Plane of Sky Early Raid Targets

> Last updated 2026-04-15 for `TextQuest#1592`.
>
> Scope: Velious-start, Free Trade, Randomized Loot, 36-character Frostreaver roster.
>
> Evidence note: static encounter and keying data comes from classic/TLP references. Randomized-loot behavior and Free Trade assumptions come from official Mischief/Teek rules plus current TLP market snapshots. Exact randomized buckets and exact Krono prices are not published by Daybreak, so bucket peers and value bands below are informed estimates rather than guaranteed tables.

## Executive Summary

- **36 accounts is enough** for both targets. Plane of Hate is the easier weekly clear; Plane of Sky is the higher-operator-friction target because keying and island sequencing matter more than raw DPS.
- **Plane of Sky is the better epic-farm priority** because it gates or supplies multiple 1.0 lines at once: ranger, monk, necromancer, shadow knight, warrior, paladin, and magician all touch Sky in some form.
- **Plane of Hate is still worth a lockout** because randomized classic raid loot turns each Hate named into another pull on the same broad classic raid bucket, while also retaining native epic-value items tied to Innoruuk / Hate.
- **Best cadence:** run Sky first each reset while keys are organized and the raid is fresh, then run Hate on a second night. If lockouts are separate but same-length, staggering them is better than stacking them into one marathon session.

## Why These Two Zones Matter Before NToV/ST

Plane of Hate and Plane of Sky are the last genuinely efficient pre-NToV classic targets for a Velious-start roster that already plans to operate six full groups. Hate is the lower-friction value farm: zone in, break, clear named, leave with classic-raid bucket pulls. Sky is the higher-friction, higher-strategic-value farm: key chain, island sequencing, Death Touch management, and several epic bottlenecks in one zone.

For raid-economics planning, that means:

- **Sky** is the better progression raid because it accelerates epics and creates the most sellable bottleneck pieces or MQ services.
- **Hate** is the better filler raid because it is easier to execute repeatedly and still throws classic raid-tier random loot at a Free Trade market.

## Plane of Hate Under Randomized Loot

### What random loot changes

Official Mischief/Teek rules state that rare NPCs draw from other rares of similar level within the same expansion, and raid NPCs draw from other raids of similar level within the same expansion. For Hate, that means the question is not "which single Hate mob drops the item" so much as "how many classic raid-bucket pulls does one clear produce?"

### Practical Hate loot map

| Hate target class | Native value that still matters | Randomized-loot implication | Frostreaver verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Break-in trash | Trash loot, class armor, incidental quest pieces | Do **not** assume ordinary trash rolls the classic raid bucket; value comes from access and filler, not jackpot odds | Clear what is needed for control and key recovery, but do not treat trash as the farm target |
| Hate named / minis | Native epic-value items and planar-class loot | Each named is another classic raid-bucket spin if it is treated as a raid or rare target under random rules | Named density is the real reason to be here |
| Innoruuk | Native marquee loot plus epic relevance | Highest-confidence classic raid-bucket pull in the zone | Always kill him if the break is stable |

### Native Hate chase items that still matter

Even with randomized loot, these Hate-native items remain useful because they either keep local demand high or can themselves disperse into the broader classic raid bucket:

| Item | Why it matters | Source reference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Eye of Innoruuk` | Necromancer epic bottleneck | Project 1999 `Eye of Innoruuk` page |
| `Hand of the Maestro` | Warrior epic bottleneck | EQProgression Warrior 1.0 guide |
| `Decrepit Hide` | Shadow Knight epic bottleneck | Project 1999 `Decrepit Hide` page |
| `Shattered Emerald of Corruption` | Ranger epic bottleneck | Project 1999 `Shattered Emerald of Corruption` page |

### What the randomized bucket likely means in practice

Daybreak does not publish exact bucket boundaries, so the safest planning assumption is:

- Hate named and Innoruuk are **classic raid-tier pulls**, not "Hate-only" pulls.
- A Hate clear is competing economically with other classic raids such as Vox, Nagafen, Fear minis, Cazic-Thule, Phinigel, and Yael, because all are part of the same classic-raid value band under random-loot rules.
- That makes Hate a **volume play**: you go for the number of named and the ease of execution, not because a single named is uniquely mandatory.

## Minimum Raid Force Estimate

### Plane of Hate

EQProgression's classic raid guide describes Innoruuk as a classic-era raid encounter with Death Touch and knockback pressure. The Project 1999 strategy guide describes Hate as a full-raid planar break rather than a group target.

For a Velious-start roster at level 60:

- **Functional floor:** about **18-24** level-60 characters with real tanks, 3+ clerics, and at least one enchanter for break control.
- **Comfort floor:** **24-30** if the goal is a clean break plus Innoruuk without depending on perfect pulls.
- **Current 36-box roster verdict:** **Yes, 36 accounts can clear Hate comfortably**. Your 3 warriors, 6 clerics, 6 bards, 4 shamans, and dedicated enchanter are above the floor for classic planar content.

### Plane of Sky

Plane of Sky is less about raw HP than about logistics:

- sequential keys
- island-by-island recovery
- Death Touch handling on upper islands
- losing time when a bad key/positioning mistake causes corpses or stranded characters

For a Velious-start roster at level 60:

- **Epic-piece farm floor (islands 1-5):** about **18-24** organized characters if enough keys already exist.
- **Weekly push floor (islands 1-7 / Eye attempt):** about **24-30** because upper-island recovery and DT management punish thin rosters harder than Hate does.
- **Current 36-box roster verdict:** **Yes, 36 accounts can clear the practical Sky targets and should be able to push upper islands**, assuming key logistics are rehearsed and rez/corpse recovery is staged.

## Plane of Sky Progression Map

The Project 1999 Plane of Sky guide and EQProgression's key guide both show Sky as a sequential island/key climb. That matters operationally more than the raw boss stats.

| Island | Operational role | Why it matters to this issue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Entry/bootstrap | Starts the weekly chain and hosts the Thunder Spirit Princess step used to trigger the necromancer Gkzzallk path |
| 2 | Early progression stop | Earliest real necromancer bottleneck (`Silver Disc`) and one of the easiest partial-farm checkpoints |
| 3 | Mid-chain farm stop | Necromancer progression continues here and failed recovery starts becoming annoying rather than trivial |
| 4 | Shared epic bottleneck island | Ranger, Shadow Knight, Necromancer, and Paladin demand all touch this layer in some form |
| 5 | Melee epic bottleneck island | Monk and Warrior pressure both land here, which makes it the most obviously monetizable melee stop |
| 6 | Quest-room / access continuation | More about controlled progression and class turn-ins than a single jackpot drop |
| 7 | Eye of Veeshan end-cap push | Marks the weekly "did we do a real Sky night or just an epic farm?" cutoff |
| 8 | Post-Eye quest room | Important for quest flow, not the first-week economic focus |

From the zone guide plus individual epic references, Sky is materially relevant to:

That is why Sky is better described as a **shared epic infrastructure zone** than as a normal raid-only loot target.

## Farming Rotation and Lockout Planning

### Likely lockout behavior

Official TLP support for classic raiding is built around Agents of Change. Community lockout references consistently describe classic AoC lockouts as separate expedition lockouts on roughly a weekly cadence.

The safest Frostreaver planning assumption is:

- **Plane of Hate and Plane of Sky should be treated as separate lockouts**
- the cadence is **probably the same weekly length**, not meaningfully different
- the operational difference is therefore not timer length, but **setup cost**

This still needs live confirmation on Frostreaver, because the exact expedition timer and whether open-world resets differ is server-side behavior.

### Recommended weekly rotation

| Window | Target | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reset night / prime raid slot | Plane of Sky | Highest coordination overhead, highest epic leverage, hardest place to recover from sloppy play |
| Second raid night | Plane of Hate | Faster clear, easier reset recovery, still a strong classic raid-bucket farm |
| Off-night opportunism | Open-world Hate/Sky checks if available | Only if it does not disrupt the main weekly keyed run |

### Priority order inside Sky

If the goal is epic profit rather than bragging-rights clearing:

1. **Island 4 first** for Ranger + Shadow Knight overlap and Paladin service value.
2. **Island 5 second** for Monk + Warrior overlap.
3. **Necromancer package pieces** during the same pass rather than as a separate special trip.
4. Push to Eye / full clear only after the bottleneck islands are already locked in for the week.

## Free Trade Krono Value Estimate

### What can actually be estimated

Daybreak does not publish bazaar data, and Frostreaver-specific prices do not exist yet. The best available analogs are:

- current Teek / recent TLP marketplace posts showing Sky bottleneck demand
- a 2025 Fangbreaker ECTunnel post listing `Swirling Sphere of Color` at **1 Krono**
- current Teek WTB interest for `Cloak of Spiroc Feathers`
- full-epic market posts showing that bottleneck pieces are sold as either single items or MQ bundles when the server rules allow it

That supports **price bands**, not precise point estimates.

### Directional value bands

| Sky item or service | Expected early Free Trade value | Reasoning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Island 4 drops (`Swirling Sphere of Color`, `Blade of Abrogation`) | **0.75-2 Krono each** in the high-demand window | Two popular melee / hybrid epics touch the same island, and one comparable TLP post already showed the ranger piece at 1 Krono |
| Island 5 drops (`Breath of Gwan`, `Spiroc Wingblade`) | **0.5-1.5 Krono each** | Monk demand should keep `Breath of Gwan` at the top of the band; warrior demand is real but narrower |
| Necromancer package pieces | **0.25-0.75 Krono per piece**, or **1-2+ Krono** as a bundled service | More moving parts, but the bundle is annoying enough that convenience sells |
| Paladin / Magician Sky services | **Service-priced rather than piece-priced** | These are better sold as keyed assistance or MQ time than as a stable bazaar commodity |

### What this means for raid economics

- **Sky is the better Krono zone** if the objective is epic monetization.
- **Hate is the better baseline value zone** if the objective is reliable weekly loot volume.
- The best combined plan is therefore **Sky for bottlenecks, Hate for bucket spins**.

## Final Recommendation

- Treat **Plane of Sky as the first weekly classic raid target** until the roster's own epic needs are satisfied and the market for Island 4 / Island 5 pieces starts to soften.
- Treat **Plane of Hate as the second weekly classic target** because it is easier to execute and still monetizes well under randomized classic raid buckets.
- Assume **36 accounts is enough for both**. The risk is not DPS or healing throughput; it is operator discipline, key distribution, and corpse recovery in Sky.
- If only one classic raid fits into the weekly schedule, choose **Sky first** while epic bottlenecks still convert into Krono.
