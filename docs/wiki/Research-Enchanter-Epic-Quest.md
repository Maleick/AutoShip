# Enchanter Epic Quest Research

*Research date: 2026-04-15*
*Issue: TextQuest#1585*

This page evaluates the Enchanter 1.0 epic, [Staff of the Serpent](https://wiki.project1999.com/Staff_of_the_Serpent), for the current [Frostreaver farming plan](Frostreaver-Farming-Guide). The goal is not to restate the quest blindly; it is to answer whether the quest is worth prioritizing on a Velious-start, free-trade, randomized-loot TLP with one enchanter supporting a melee-heavy 36-box roster.

## Executive Summary

- The epic is **not** the only pre-Luclin way to obtain haste, but it **is** the only reusable pre-Luclin item click that delivers long-duration, near-top-end spell haste from an unlimited-charge item. [Speed of the Shissar](https://wiki.project1999.com/Speed_of_the_Shissar) is a 30-minute, single-target, 66% haste buff with extra AC and AGI.
- Jeff's shorthand "`permanent haste click`" is directionally right for ownership and reuse, but the buff itself is **not permanent**. The item is permanent; each buff lasts 30 minutes.
- Randomized loot helps on the named and raid-drop segments, especially `Shining Metallic Robes`, `Vessel Drozlin`, `Verina Tomb`, `Prince Selrach Di`zok`, `The Tangrin`, and the Plane of Fear / Plane of Hate drops. It does **not** remove the fixed bottlenecks: ground spawns, charm hand-ins, sack combines, or Jeb's Seal progression.
- MQ value is **low**. Free trade and randomized buckets matter much more than classic multiquesting because the key epic masters mostly want a single combined sack, not several separate tradeable turn-in items.
- For Frostreaver's roster, the enchanter epic looks like a **high-value single-character epic**, but not the first epic overall. Current priority is:
  1. Cleric
  2. Bard
  3. Enchanter
  4. Monk
  5. Mage

## Server Assumptions

This assessment assumes the Frostreaver ruleset already documented elsewhere in the wiki:

- [Frostreaver Farming Guide](Frostreaver-Farming-Guide): Velious start, free trade, randomized loot, no truebox, melee-heavy 36-box roster with **one** enchanter and **many** melee beneficiaries.
- Official Mischief / Thornblade rules language from Daybreak: rare NPC loot is randomized across similar rare NPCs in the same expansion, raid loot is randomized across similar raid NPCs in the same expansion, and rare NPCs spawn more often.

## Randomized Loot Impact

Daybreak's published rule is broad, but it is **not** item-by-item. The table below is therefore a **bucket inference**, not a guaranteed Frostreaver drop list. The logic is:

- `High`: named or raid-drop step that should materially benefit if Frostreaver mirrors Mischief-style rare / raid randomization
- `Medium`: named drop that may randomize, but classification is murky or bucket membership is likely narrower
- `None`: fixed quest action, ground spawn, charm hand-in, or combine that randomized loot does not solve

| `Innoruuk's Word` | `Verina Tomb`, Neriak | Medium | Same story as Vessel: classic named bottleneck, likely rare-bucket candidate |
| `Head of a Prince` | `Prince Selrach Di`zok`, Chardok | High | Explicit raid / raid-like named target on a 2-hour timer; best candidate for bucket relief |

### What Randomized Loot Does Not Solve

- `Jeb's Seal` remains mandatory because the enchanter masters still require sack combines and hand-ins.
- The [Enchanter Epic Quest](https://wiki.project1999.com/Enchanter_Epic_Quest) still forces travel across Erudin, Ak`Anon, Burning Woods, Oggok, Plane of Sky, Firiona Vie, The Hole, Plane of Fear, and Plane of Hate.
- Charm execution still matters for the gem stage even if named-drop steps become easier.
- Ground-spawn timers still matter, especially the Plane of Sky book.

## Pre-Luclin Haste Click Validation

The [Haste Guide](https://wiki.project1999.com/Haste_Guide) is the best single source for validating what makes the enchanter epic special.

### What the Staff Actually Provides

- [Staff of the Serpent](https://wiki.project1999.com/Staff_of_the_Serpent): must equip, level 50 click, 6.0 second cast.
- [Speed of the Shissar](https://wiki.project1999.com/Speed_of_the_Shissar): single-target, 30-minute buff, 66% haste, +40 AGI, +12 AC.

### Why It Still Matters Even Though Enchanters Have Better Spells

At level 58, enchanters can cast `Wonderous Rapidity`, which is stronger raw haste than `Speed of the Shissar`. That does **not** make the epic irrelevant on Frostreaver:

- The click is mana-free.
- The click is item-based, so one enchanter can prebuff melee groups out of combat without dedicating spell gems or mana cycles to haste refreshes.
- Frostreaver's published roster is melee-heavy, so one enchanter's utility spreads across many monks, warriors, paladins, bard groups, and melee pets.

### Alternative Pre-Luclin Haste Clicks

| Item | Era | Type | Why it is not the same as enchanter epic |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Eyepatch of Plunder` | Velious | 20% click haste, 10 minutes | Much weaker, short duration |
| `Ashenwood Short Spear` | Classic | 35% click haste, 10 charges | Consumable charges, melee classes only |
| `Clockwork Watchman Vambraces` | Classic | 40% click haste, 10 charges | Charges, class and race locked |
| `Celestial Fists` | Monk epic | 40% click haste, 18 seconds | Self-DPS tool, not a general buff platform |
| `Dark Cloak of the Sky` | Ranger quest item | 50% click haste, 18 seconds | Short combat click, ranger-only |
| Potion line: `Flurry` / `Adrenaline Tap` | Velious | 20-40% expendable haste | Consumables, not reusable permanent gear |

### Bottom Line

If "`only permanent haste click`" means "**only unlimited-charge, long-duration pre-Luclin haste click worth maintaining across a roster**," the claim holds up. If it means "**literally the only item in era that can ever click haste**," it does not.

## MQ Viability

MQ viability is **low**.

Why:

- Each master stage collapses four ingredients into a **single combined sack** before the turn-in.
- The final hand-in to Jeb is a **single `Bundle of Staves`**.
- Single-item hand-ins do not create the same high-leverage MQ shortcuts that cleric, bard, or mage communities often use for staged turn-ins.
- Many of the meaningful shortcuts are not MQ at all; they are:
  - free-trade purchases of tradeable subcomponents
  - randomized named / raid buckets producing the right drop on an easier target
  - guild / box-team assistance on Fear, Hate, Chardok, and Plane of Sky access

There are still narrow MQ-like edge cases on earlier multi-item turn-ins, but they do not change the overall verdict. Frostreaver should treat this as a **farm-and-buy** problem, not a **buy-an-MQ** problem.

## Timeline Estimate

The quest's clock is driven by a few specific timers:

- [`Vessel Drozlin`](https://wiki.project1999.com/Vessel_Drozlin): 2-7 days random respawn
- [`Verina Tomb`](https://wiki.project1999.com/Verina_Tomb): 2-4 days random respawn
- [`Wraith of a Shissir`](https://wiki.project1999.com/Wraith_of_a_Shissir): 72 hours or zone repop
- [`The Tangrin`](https://wiki.project1999.com/The_Tangrin): 48 hours
- [`Prince Selrach Di`zok`](https://wiki.project1999.com/Prince_Selrach_Di%27zok): 2 hours
- [`Ghost of Kindle`](https://wiki.project1999.com/The_Ghost_of_Kindle): 21 minutes 30 seconds
- [Enchanter Epic Quest](https://wiki.project1999.com/Enchanter_Epic_Quest): `Book of Charm and Sacrifice` ground spawn every 24 hours

### Best-Case Calendar Time

**4-10 days**

This assumes:

- raid access to Fear / Hate / Sky
- Frostreaver random buckets actually helping on the named-drop steps
- tradeable subcomponents bought instead of farmed where possible
- the enchanter is already level 60 with the travel network ready

### Realistic Self-Found Calendar Time

**2-4 weeks**

This assumes:

- you are personally waiting on `Vessel Drozlin` / `Verina Tomb` windows
- you still need a Plane of Sky book cycle
- you are farming at least one or two Plane of Fear / Hate steps directly
- you are doing the charm stage yourself instead of paying for help

### Worst-Case Calendar Time

**4+ weeks**

This is the classic outcome if one or more of the following go wrong:

- repeated misses on Vessel / Verina windows
- no guild control of Fear / Hate
- no helpful randomized bucket outcome on Tangrin / Prince / Plane targets
- failed charm-stage execution or faction friction in Firiona Vie / The Hole

## Effort / Value Comparison

This section is partly source-backed and partly roster inference. The item effects come from the linked epic pages; the rank order reflects Frostreaver's actual 36-box composition.

| Epic | What you gain | Quest pain point | Frostreaver verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Cleric epic](https://wiki.project1999.com/Water_Sprinkler_of_Nem_Ankh) | 96% rez click, still raid-defining | Long quest, but the payoff is immediate and always relevant | **Best overall value** because six clerics touch every raid night |
| [Bard epic](https://wiki.project1999.com/Bard_Epic_Quest) | `Dance of the Blade`, group combat haste / stat tool | Long, multi-raid instrument quest | **Second-best value** because six bards amplify every melee group |
| [Enchanter epic](https://wiki.project1999.com/Staff_of_the_Serpent) | Mana-free 30-minute 66% haste click for roster prebuffing | Multiple named timers plus fixed quest friction | **Third** because one enchanter can still improve many melee bodies |
| [Monk epic](https://wiki.project1999.com/Monk_Epic_Quest) | `Celestial Fists`, self-DPS spike plus 9/16 fists | Moderate-high quest complexity, but repeatable per monk | **Fourth** because one monk epic is excellent, but eight monks dilute the early marginal gain |
| [Mage epic](https://wiki.project1999.com/Kurrat%27s_Magician_Epic_Guide) | `Summon Orb` / epic pet platform | `Staff of Elemental Mastery: Earth` is notorious | **Fifth** because the mage count is low and the gating item is worse than the payoff |

### Recommendation

For Frostreaver's actual roster, the enchanter epic should be treated as a **premium single-character utility epic**:

- higher priority than mage epic
- competitive with the first monk epic
- behind cleric and bard epics if the goal is earliest raid-wide power

If the goal is to maximize one character's unique utility early, the enchanter epic is worth the pain. If the goal is to raise aggregate raid-floor performance as fast as possible, cleric and bard stay ahead.
