# Research: Dozekar Tear Quest Items -- Free Trade BiS Strategy

Research for `TextQuest#1586`. Focus: how Velious Dozekar tear quests change on a free-trade TLP where the completed rewards can be sold instead of staying no-drop.

## Executive Summary

- The Dozekar line is more valuable on free-trade than on standard loot rules because the completed rewards stop being personal progression pieces and become tradeable raid-era commodities.
- The Project 1999 quest matrix currently shows **13** Dozekar-related rewards, not 11.
- The best economic targets are the rewards gated by the rare tears and the single-source symbols:
  - `Silver Bracelet of Speed`
  - `White Dragon Idol`
  - `Silver Charm of Tranquility`
  - `Pauldrons of the Deep Flame`
  - `Cloak of the Fire Storm`
  - `Serrated Dragon Tooth`
- The real bottlenecks are not the common tears. They are:
  - `Platinum Tear`, `Ruby Tear`, and `Serrated Tear` from Dozekar
  - `Serrated Symbol` and `Ruby Symbol` from `Lepethida`
  - `Silver Symbol` and `Platinum Symbol` from `Essedera`
- On a server with Agents of Change, the risk shifts from "can we get the kill?" to "can we beat the rest of the market to launch-week demand before prices compress?"

## Why Free Trade Changes The Quest

On a normal ruleset, Dozekar rewards are no-drop and their value is mostly internal to the raid that loots them. On Frostreaver-style free trade, three separate things become marketable:

1. The tear itself
2. The symbol or drake-orb gate item
3. The finished reward item

That creates a new monetization path that does not exist on standard servers:

- Rare tears can be sold as stand-alone bottlenecks
- Full multiquests become a convenience premium product
- Finished BiS or near-BiS rewards can be sold directly to buyers who do not want to manage faction, timers, or MQ coordination

### 36-Box Roster Fit

Against the current Frostreaver farm / raid roster in [Frostreaver Farming Guide](Frostreaver-Farming-Guide.md), the internal demand profile is heavily skewed toward melee:

- `Silver Bracelet of Speed`, `Cloak of the Fire Storm`, `Silver Mask of the Slayer`, `Pauldrons of the Deep Flame`, and `Serrated Dragon Tooth` all hit the 3 WAR / 1 SK / 2 PAL / 6 BRD / 8 MNK / 1 RNG buyer pool.
- `White Dragon Idol`, `Mask of the Silver Eyes`, `Buckler of Insight`, and `Silver Charm of Tranquility` cover the 6 CLR / 4 SHM / 1 DRU / 1 ENC / 1 WIZ / 1 MAG support and caster pool.

That makes the tear line attractive both as external bazaar stock and as a direct internal gearing funnel.

1. Explicit historical Mangler prices for tears, symbols, and three full MQ rewards
2. The thread's own implied conversion of `$10 ~= 1 Krono`
3. Aradune evidence that Dozekar full MQ service still existed later, even on a non-free-trade TLP
4. Existing Mischief / randomized-loot guidance in [Frostreaver Farming Guide](Frostreaver-Farming-Guide.md), which already notes that launch-window prices compress sharply once supply ramps

### Trading read

- **Sell finished rewards early** when the item has a clear demand premium above the component floor:
  - `Silver Bracelet of Speed`
  - `White Dragon Idol`
  - `Silver Charm of Tranquility`
  - `Cloak of the Fire Storm`

- Expect the launch premium to decay quickly once weekly instance clears accumulate.

## Farming Rotation

### Rotation goal

Do not optimize for "most kills." Optimize for "most bottlenecks cleared per reset window."

### Recommended order

1. **Track `Lepethida` and `Essedera` first**
   - These are the only sources for four single-source symbols:
     - `Serrated Symbol`
     - `Ruby Symbol`
     - `Silver Symbol`
     - `Platinum Symbol`
   - Those four symbols gate the highest-value finished items.

2. **Kill `Dozekar the Cursed` every cycle**
   - Dozekar is the only stable source for the three rare tears that define the top-end market:
     - `Platinum Tear`
     - `Ruby Tear`
     - `Serrated Tear`

3. **Fill in `Grozzmel` / `Midayor` next**
   - Their dual-source symbols feed both the caster and melee lines:
     - `Emerald Symbol`
     - `Runed Symbol`

4. **Fill in `Casalen` / `Tavekalem` after that**
   - These dual-source symbols stabilize the middle of the market:
     - `Poison Symbol`
     - `White Symbol`

5. **Finish with `Krigara` / `Ymmeln`**
   - These symbols matter, but they are not the hard supply wall:
     - `Black Symbol`
     - `Flame Kissed Symbol`

### 36-box operating pattern

- Use the SK-led driver group as the first-tag pull group for open-world contention and wing cleanup.
- Use two to four melee groups to hold the drake wings and keep the clear path safe.
- Keep the ranger / druid utility group free to scout timers and cover movement between open-world pulls.
- Use the caster / utility group to stabilize awkward pathing, recover from wipes, and keep summon / port logistics out of the critical path.

### Inventory policy

- Bank all rare tears and single-source symbols until you can either:
  - complete one of the S-tier rewards, or
  - sell the bottleneck into an obvious shortage
- Avoid cashing common tears into low-tier finished items during the launch window if those same common tears can help finish a higher-margin sell.

## Competition Risk

### Open-world competition

| Target | Risk | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Essedera` | Very high | Only source for `Silver Symbol` and `Platinum Symbol`. |
| `Lepethida` | Very high | Only source for `Serrated Symbol` and `Ruby Symbol`. |
| `Dozekar the Cursed` | High | Only stable source for the three rare tears. |
| `Grozzmel` / `Midayor` | Medium | Important symbols, but each has two sources. |
| `Casalen` / `Tavekalem` | Medium | Two-source symbols and lower resale pressure. |
| `Krigara` / `Ymmeln` | Medium-low | Useful, but not where the rare-money bottlenecks sit. |

### AoC-adjusted competition

If Frostreaver behaves like a modern TLP with Agents of Change, the competitive picture changes:

- **Spawn denial risk drops**
- **Price compression risk rises**

That means the best income window is usually the first few instance cycles, before every organized guild starts converting weekly clears into bazaar stock.

### Practical read

- In open world, encounter locking helps once you land the first tag, but it does not remove timer races.
- In instanced content, the trade is not "fight for the mob" but "beat other farmers to the first two to four weeks of demand."
- The highest competition will cluster around the exact bottlenecks that map to the best launch items:
  - `Silver Bracelet of Speed`
  - `White Dragon Idol`
  - `Silver Charm of Tranquility`
  - `Pauldrons of the Deep Flame`

## Recommendation

For free-trade monetization, treat the Dozekar line as a **bottleneck-conversion business**, not just a dragon kill.

- Farm `Essedera`, `Lepethida`, and `Dozekar` on every cycle.
- Build finished items only when the completed reward beats the part-out price.
- Prioritize the melee haste and priest range items first.
- Use the common-symbol drakes to complete profitable sets, not as the main source of value.
